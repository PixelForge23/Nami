Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Nami.

What digital marketing services do you offer?
We offer a full suite of digital marketing services including website design, SEO, Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Instagram marketing, social media management, brand identity, graphic design, video editing, email marketing, content marketing, lead generation, local SEO, Google Business Profile optimisation, WhatsApp marketing, and AI video creation.
How much do your services cost?
Our pricing is customised based on your specific needs and goals. We offer flexible packages for businesses of all sizes. Contact us for a free consultation and we'll provide a tailored quote that fits your budget.
How long does it take to see results?
Results vary depending on the service. Some campaigns show initial results within 2-4 weeks, while SEO and brand building typically show significant results within 3-6 months. We provide regular reports and keep you updated on progress.
Do you work with businesses outside India?
Yes, we work with clients globally. Our team is experienced in serving businesses across different markets and time zones.
What makes Nami different from other agencies?
Nami combines data-driven strategies with creative excellence. We're committed to transparency, regular reporting, and a partnership approach that focuses on your long-term growth.
How do you measure success?
We track key performance indicators (KPIs) aligned with your business goals — from website traffic and conversion rates to lead generation and ROI.
Do you offer ongoing support after the campaign?
Absolutely. We believe in building long-term partnerships. We provide ongoing support, optimisation, and strategic guidance.
Can you help with local SEO?
Yes, we specialise in local SEO services. We optimise your Google Business Profile, manage local citations, and implement strategies to help you rank higher in local search results.
What is the onboarding process like?
We start with a discovery session to understand your goals, then conduct a full audit of your current digital presence, and finally develop a custom strategy plan before launching any campaigns.
Do you provide content for social media?
Yes, our content marketing team creates engaging social media content, including graphics, videos, captions, and stories. We ensure your brand voice is consistent across all platforms.
